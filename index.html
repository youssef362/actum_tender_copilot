Describe your need in a chat, or upload a draft. The copilot writes the tender file in Arabic and English, checks it against the Qatar Tenders Law, proposes evaluation criteria and emails you the PDFs.

How it works

  1. DescribeDiscuss your need with the copilot, or attach documents you already have; it asks for anything missing, such as locations, scope and contract term.
  2. DraftIt writes the scope, specifications, terms and bill of quantities in Arabic and English.
  3. CheckEvery file is checked against the Qatar Tenders Law and its regulations.
  4. EvaluateCriteria, weights and a scoring guide, ready for the committee.
  5. PackageYou receive one PDF by email: the bilingual tender file and its readiness report.
